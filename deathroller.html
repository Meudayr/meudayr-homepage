How to Download & Install

Built for World of Warcraft: Forever (interface 1.60.1). Both players must have the addon installed to play.

Option 1: CurseForge App (Recommended)

  1. Open the CurseForge desktop app and select your WoW Forever installation.
  2. Search for Doctor Gup's Deathroller (or Project ID 1803451).
  3. Click Install. CurseForge will place it in your AddOns folder and handle updates automatically.
  4. If WoW is already running, type /reload in chat to load the addon.

Option 2: Manual Zip Install

  1. Visit the CurseForge Files page and download the latest DoctorGupsDeathroller-*.zip release.
  2. Extract the archive into your WoW Forever Interface\AddOns\ folder.
  3. Verify the folder contains the .toc file directly (not nested inside an extra folder).
  4. Launch the game (or run /reload) and confirm it is enabled in the AddOns menu.

How to Play

Target a player, name your stakes, and roll your way down from 100. Whoever rolls a 1 loses the pot.

1

Challenge Your Opponent

Target another player nearby and type /dr (or /gup). If you cannot target them directly, run /dr challenge First Last.

2

Pick Your Wager

Type a gold amount or use one of the quick picks (10, 50, 100, 500, 1,000), or leave it at 0 for a friendly game. Before sending, the addon verifies that your opponent has Doctor Gup's Deathroller and a compatible version.

3

Opponent Accepts

Your opponent receives a popup showing the pot, your head-to-head record against each other, and an accept countdown.

4

Take Turns Rolling Down

Type /roll N in the window's roll box, where N is the last number rolled (the opening roll is /roll 100). Every roll is read from the game's real roll message—rolls out of turn or with the wrong range are ignored. Dice spin on both screens before the result lands.

5

Pay Out via the Trade Window

Roll a 1 and you lose. The result screen shows who owes whom, milestones, and rematch/announce buttons. When you open a trade with someone you owe (or who owes you), a banner displays the debt, tracks gold going in, warns if it's short, gives a one-click Put in button, and clears the debt when the trade completes.

Key Features

Built for WoW Forever

Supports Forever's first-and-last-name characters (First Last) and modern addon rules out of the box.

Verified Rolls & Suspense

Reads real /roll system messages and ignores wrong-range or out-of-turn rolls. Keeps rolls out of chat while the dice spin on both screens.

Stats, History & Debts

Open /dr stats for your win/loss record, net gold, streaks, biggest win, searchable 1,000-game history (hover to see every roll), and debt ledger with Mark Paid and Forgive.

Trade Window Banner

Appears only when trading someone with an active deathroll debt. Tracks gold in real time and offers a one-click Put in button.

No Chat Spam

Addons communicate via hidden addon messages. Optional emotes for challenges, streaks, and big pots only fire if you enable them—nothing goes to /say, party, or guild chat.

Minimap & Options

Left-click the minimap button for your game or stats; right-click for options (auto-focus roll box, pre-fill /roll, sounds, window size).

Slash Commands

Command What It Does
/dr or /gup With a player targeted: challenge them. Otherwise: show your active game or stats.
/dr challenge First Last Challenge a player by name when you cannot target them directly.
/roll N Roll your turn (can also be typed in the addon window's roll box).
/dr forfeit Forfeit your current game, or cancel a pending challenge.
/dr stats Open or close the Stats, History, and Debts window.
/dr options Open the addon options window.
/dr minimap Show or hide the minimap button.
/dr version Display your installed addon version.

Good to Know

  • Both players need the addon: It checks compatibility before sending a challenge and warns you if the other player is offline, busy, or on an incompatible version.
  • Stand close or group up: System roll messages only reach nearby players and group members.
  • Honor system: The addon never moves gold on its own—trades still require both players to accept.
