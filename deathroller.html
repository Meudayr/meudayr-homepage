How to Play

Five steps, lil gup. Click through the walkthrough instead of scrolling forever.

1. Challenge a player

Target a player and type /dr (or /gup). If targeting a moving character is too mechanical for you, use /dr challenge First Last. Want to farm any brave gup nearby without picking a target? Type /dr open [gold] and the first player to type /roll 100 within 60 seconds takes the bait.

Grouped up in a party or raid? You can challenge from anywhere in Azeroth. Flying solo? Stand close enough to see each other's rolls.

2. Pick your wager

Type an amount or mash a quick pick (10, 50, 100, 500, 1,000), or leave it at 0 if you're broke. Before sending, the add-on checks if your opponent has Doctor Gup's Deathroller. If that gup hasn't installed it yet, the button switches to Challenge in chat so they can still play by typing /roll 100.

3. Opponent accepts

Add-on players accept from a popup showing the pot, your head-to-head record, and a 60-second countdown, then a 50/50 coin flip picks who rolls first. Chat-challenged gups skip the coin flip and accept by typing /roll 100 as their opening roll.

4. Take turns rolling

Take turns typing /roll N in the window's roll box, where N is the last number rolled. The first roll is /roll 100. Don't improvise. If an add-onless gup panics and goes quiet for 2 minutes, you can end the game with no result.

5. Roll a 1 and settle up

Roll a 1 and you lose, gup. The result screen shows who owes whom, any milestones, and buttons to rematch, go double or nothing, or announce the beatdown to Emote, Party, or Guild.

Open a trade with someone you owe (or who owes you) and a banner above the trade window tells you the exact gold to type into the trade box, tracks the gold going in live, warns if it's short, and clears the debt when the trade completes.

Free For All & Live Spectating

Clean out a whole tavern at once, or sweat someone else's gold from across the map.

Free For All (/dr ffa)

One host, any number of degens, and nobody else in the pile even needs the add-on installed.

  • Run /dr ffa [roll] (like /dr ffa 150) or click Free For All in your stats window. Your character emotes the lobby, and anyone nearby types 1 in chat to jump in (-1 to bail out).
  • Click Start rolling (or let the 60-second timer finish) to call everyone in by name to /roll N. Each player's first roll in range counts, and you can click Close rolls early if a slow gup wanders off. The lowest roller pays the highest roller the difference: roll 120 at the top and 10 at the bottom, and the bottom gup owes the winner 110g.
  • Ties for highest or lowest force just the tied players to re-roll /roll N to settle who wins or pays, while the gold owed still uses the original rolls. If the whole lobby rolls the same number, nobody owes a copper.
  • Anyone in the lobby running the add-on gets hidden whispers when they join, a ready-check alert when rolls open, and automatic stats, history (tagged free for all), and debt tracking when they win or pay. If some sneaky jonboy hosts a forged result that doesn't match the roll you made, your add-on throws it out.

Live Spectating (/dr watch)

Watch any 1v1 duel or Free For All live from anywhere in Azeroth without spoiling the rolls.

  • Target a player and type /dr watch (or /dr watch First Last), or open the Live tab in /dr stats to see every game running in your guild or group and click one to tune in.
  • 1v1 spectators get a read-only game window with both players, the pot, the live Chance to Win bar, close-call alarms, the roll ladder, and the spinning dice reveal. System roll lines stay out of your chat until the dice land, and joining mid-game catches you up on the rolls so far. Free For All spectators watch players join, rolls drop live, tie-breaks play out, and who owes whom.
  • Up to 10 spectators can watch a game. Watching is silent, records nothing to your own stats or debts, and stops the moment you close the window.
  • Players in a match see 2 watching in their window header with spectator names on mouseover, so you always know which nosey jonboy is sweating your pot. Uncheck Let others watch your games in Options to lock the doors and hide from the Live list.

Features & Captain's Ledger

Everything a real gamer needs to track gold, rolls, and unpaid IOUs.

Stats Window (/dr stats)

Switch between the four ledger tabs just like inside the add-on, or launch an Open challenge or Free For All straight from the bottom bar:

Overview: Your win/loss record, net gold, streaks, biggest win, and every opponent you've farmed (or fed).

History: Your last 1,000 games (including matches tagged free for all, no add-on, or forfeit), searchable and filterable by opponent, wins or losses, and time period. Hover any row to inspect the rolls.

Debts: Who owes you and who you owe across 1v1s and Free For Alls, with Mark paid and Forgive (for when a lil gup begs hard enough).

Live: Active 1v1 duels and Free For All lobbies happening right now in your guild or group, complete with stakes and roll ranges. Click any row to watch live from anywhere in the world.

Announcements & Enter Prompt

The result screen's Announce button has an arrow menu to flex your win (and milestones like streaks or your biggest pot) to Emote (people nearby), Party (or Raid), or Guild. Since WoW Forever 1.60.1 requires a key press after a mouse click to post in chat, clicking an announcement dims the window and shows a brass confirmation box with a colored preview: press Enter to post or Esc to skip.

Options

Auto-focus the roll box, pre-fill /roll, pick where result announcements go, toggle Let others watch your games, and adjust close-call effects, sounds, and window size in its own window or under Options > AddOns.

Minimap Button

Left-click for the game or stats, right-click for options, drag to move. Also lives in the add-on compartment.

/dr version Check your version before blaming the add-on.

Good to Know

  • Opponents don't even need the add-on anymore. When both of you have it, you get the challenge popup, 50/50 coin flip, and synced dice reveal. If the other gup doesn't have it, use Challenge in chat, /dr open, or /dr ffa and your add-on referees their system /roll lines straight from chat.
  • Group up or stand close to play (spectate from anywhere). To read system /roll lines in a match, you need to either be in a party/raid together or stand near each other. Spectators using /dr watch or the Live tab get fed rolls over hidden add-on whispers, so they can watch from another continent.
  • It never moves gold on its own. WoW doesn't let add-ons fill in trade gold for you. The trade banner tells you how much to type into the gold box, tracks it live, and settles the debt when both players click accept.
  • No uninvited chat spam. Add-ons coordinate and stream spectator data via hidden add-on messages. Public emotes and party/guild victory announcements only fire when you ask for them.
