How to Download & Install

Built for World of Warcraft: Forever (interface 1.60.1). Both players must have the add-on installed to play.

Option 1: CurseForge App (Recommended)

  1. Open the CurseForge desktop app and select your WoW Forever installation.
  2. Search for Doctor Gup's Deathroller (or Project ID 1803451).
  3. Click Install. CurseForge will place it in your AddOns folder and handle updates automatically.
  4. If WoW is already running, type /reload in chat to load the add-on.

Option 2: Manual Zip Install

  1. Visit the CurseForge Files page and download the latest DoctorGupsDeathroller-*.zip release.
  2. Extract the archive into your WoW Forever Interface\AddOns\ folder.
  3. Verify the folder contains the .toc file directly (not nested inside an extra folder).
  4. Launch the game (or run /reload) and confirm it is enabled in the AddOns menu.

Why Gup's?

Made for Forever

Works with Forever's first-and-last-name characters and its modern add-on rules.

Rolls you can trust

Every roll is a real /roll, read from the game's own roll message. Rolls out of turn or with the wrong range are ignored, so nobody can fake a result.

Real suspense

The roll stays out of chat while the dice spin on both players' screens, then the number lands.

Stakes front and center

The gold pot sits in the middle of the window, big and gold, from the challenge to the result.

Debts that actually get paid

Open a trade with someone you owe, or who owes you, and a banner shows the amount and tracks the gold as it goes in. The debt clears when the trade completes.

How to Play

Target a player, type /dr, name your stakes, and roll your way down from 100.

1

Challenge a player

Target a player and type /dr (or /gup). If you cannot target them directly, use /dr challenge First Last.

2

Pick your wager

Type an amount or use a quick pick (10, 50, 100, 500, 1,000), or leave it at 0 for a friendly game. Before you send, the add-on checks that your opponent has Doctor Gup's Deathroller and a compatible version.

3

Opponent accepts

They accept from a popup showing the pot, your record against each other, and a countdown.

4

Take turns rolling

Take turns typing /roll N in the window's roll box, where N is the last number rolled. The first roll is /roll 100.

5

Roll a 1 and settle up

Roll a 1 and you lose. The result screen shows who owes whom, any milestones, and buttons to rematch or announce the result.

Features

Stats Window (/dr stats)

  • Overview: Your record, net gold, streaks, biggest win, and every opponent you've played.
  • History: Your last 1,000 games, searchable and filterable by opponent, wins or losses, and time period. Hover a game to see every roll.
  • Debts: Who owes you and who you owe, with Mark paid and Forgive.

Game Window

Both players, whose turn it is, the pot, the current number, a roll box, and the full roll history.

Trade Banner

Appears only when there's a deathroll debt with the person you're trading. It shows the gold going in, warns if it's short, and has a one-click Put in button when you're paying.

Announcements (Optional)

Your character can emote challenges, results, and milestones such as win streaks and your biggest pot to people nearby. Nothing goes to /say, party, or guild chat.

Options

Auto-focus the roll box, pre-fill /roll, sounds, window size, and more, in its own window or under Options > AddOns.

Minimap Button

Left-click for the game or your stats, right-click for options, drag to move. It's also in the add-on compartment.

Commands

Command What It Does
/dr or /gup With a player targeted: challenge them. Otherwise: show your game or stats.
/dr challenge First Last Challenge someone you can't target.
/roll N Roll your turn (or type it in the window's roll box).
/dr forfeit Give up, or cancel your challenge.
/dr stats Open or close the stats window.
/dr options Open the options.
/dr minimap Show or hide the minimap button.
/dr version Show your version.

Good to Know

  • Both players need the add-on. It checks before you challenge and tells you if the other player is offline, busy, or on an incompatible version.
  • Stand close or group up. Roll messages only reach nearby players and group members.
  • It never moves gold on its own. Trades still need both of you to accept, and wagers are an honor system.
  • No chat spam. The add-ons talk to each other with hidden add-on messages. Emotes only happen if you turn them on.
