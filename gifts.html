Gift Mechanics, Support Priority & Town Shops

Core Gift-Giving Rules (Spoiler-Free)

  • Weekly Limit: You can give each character 1 gift per in-game week during Free Time in Dagsion.
  • First Gift ("New Bond"): Giving any first gift to a character you have met in Dagsion establishes a New Bond. This reveals their recruitment conditions in your roster and unlocks the ability to share Meals with them, even if that first gift is not one of their favorites.
  • Reaction Tiers:
    • Loved (++, Double Golden Arrow): Grants the maximum support point bonus (roughly 2x standard points).
    • Liked (+, Single Arrow): Grants a moderate support boost and restores character Motivation.
    • Neutral / Disliked: Minimal or zero support gain after the initial New Bond gift.
  • Birthday Bonus: Any gift given on a character's birthday is automatically treated as at least Really Liked, making birthdays ideal for inexpensive items.

Where to Buy Gifts & How to Save Gold

  • Dagsion Shopping Arcade (Marketplace): Unlocks in Chapter 4. Sells higher-rarity curiosities, instruments, and accessories.
  • Dagsion Lowtown Tavern: Sells food, snacks, and drink gifts at lower prices. Inventory restocks each chapter.
  • Regional Town Shops (27 Towns): World map towns stock regional specialties (ranging from 30 G to 2,000 G). Every known town vendor location and Gold price is listed in the right-hand column of each gift row.
  • Gift Crates: Purchased crates yield random high-rarity gifts at a lower average cost than buying individual expensive items.
  • 10% Permanent Discount: Unlock the Gifts Discount Boon of Salvation for 300 Karma Shards at a Pale Raven's Perch or Shrine of Causality before buying expensive gifts in bulk.
